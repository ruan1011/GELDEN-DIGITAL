OUR STORY

It all started
on a Saturday night.

We met on a Saturday at my work, of all places — a pub and grill.

Sonica was this beautiful lady that caught my eye, and I couldn't get her out of my mind.

That night I searched through Instagram and Facebook for four hours. Eventually, I found her — and that is where it all started.

One "date-nie date-nie" and four seasons in eight hours later, and we were completely in love.

And we've never looked back since.
